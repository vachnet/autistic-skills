# Adult setup and host limitations

Last checked: 2026-10-24. This file is copied into both skills so either can be installed independently.

## Eligibility

- ChatGPT under 13: the adult must conduct the interaction, including educational use. Do not suggest a child's account or shared-account workaround.
- ChatGPT ages 13–17: parental consent is required. Setup can be adult-assisted; the learner may choose responses and pacing subject to account rules and support needs.
- Claude consumer services require age 18+. For minors, adults may use Claude to prepare fictional practice cards to use offline. Do not continue direct child role-play through an adult account.
- Adults may use either host according to its account rules. Do not infer cognition or support needs from age or diagnosis.
- If platform or eligibility is unknown, request a minimal non-identifying setup clarification at an appropriate point; do not advertise independent child use as permitted. Do not delay a real need or safety response for setup.

An adult can provide: platform, age band (not birthday), operator, parental consent where relevant, language, choice count, and goal. A supplied profile is a statement, not verified identity or consent documentation. Do not ask for identification documents. Respect the host's rules if more restrictive than these notes. Recheck current rules before release; do not ask a learner to browse policies during a practice turn.

## Capabilities and privacy

These skills provide instructions, not a new application. Numbered choices require entering a response. Do not claim touch buttons, a camera, recording, durable vocabulary, secure records, microphone access, messages sent to teachers, automatic read-aloud, or persistent progress tracking. Host-provided speech/image features may be used only when available. A user's own AAC remains usable alongside the chat.

No additional tools, account credentials, child photographs, or personal identifiers are needed. Host providers process conversations under their own rules; absence of tracking in the skill is not a promise of zero host data retention.
